The Problem

A $2T continent's logistics stack is stuck in 1995

The DRC is Sub-Saharan Africa's largest country by landmass and one of its fastest-growing populations. Yet travel and cargo still run on phone calls, intermediaries, and cash.

📞

Fragmented

Booking a flight, hotel, or moving cargo across Kinshasa–Lubumbashi means brokers, not apps.

💵

Cash-only

No single wallet, no loyalty, no programmatic reconciliation for supply partners.

🕳️

No visibility

Where is my shipment? Is my boarding pass valid? The trusted layer simply doesn't exist.

The gap is trust, not demand. Nobody has built the trusted layer. That is the company.
